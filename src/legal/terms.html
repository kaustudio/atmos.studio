What this is

Atmos Gallery reads a colour palette from an image and describes the mood it finds there. It is free, there is nothing to sign up for, and it is made by KauStudio ApS, a company registered in Denmark. Using it means accepting these terms.

Don't use Atmos Gallery for anything unlawful where you are, and don't try to break or overload it.

Your images and palettes stay yours

We claim no rights over the images you open or the palettes you make. Use them for anything, commercial work included, with no credit required.

You are responsible for having the right to use the images you open. A palette read from someone else's photograph says nothing about your right to use that photograph. Where your image goes is set out in Privacy.

A reading is an interpretation

The name and description are a reading, not a measurement, and two people can reasonably disagree with any of it. Colours come from the image data as your browser decodes it, on a display we know nothing about, so don't rely on Atmos Gallery for print production, brand matching, or anything else colour-critical.

The contrast figures work the same way: they compare two colour values in isolation and say nothing about type size, weight, opacity, or what sits behind them. They are a way to sort palettes, not an accessibility audit. Test the interface you actually shipped.

Keeping your own copies

Your library is in this browser's local storage. Clearing your browser data deletes it, and we cannot get it back. There is no server-side copy, by design. Use Back up to keep a copy of your own if a palette matters to you.

Shared links

A shared link carries the palette inside the link itself, after the #. Anyone with it can open it, you cannot recall it, and we have no record of it to delete.
